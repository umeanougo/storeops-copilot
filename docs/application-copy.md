## Application version — under 1,500 characters

While helping a third-party fulfilment operator examine its Shopify workflows, I observed a team repeatedly switching between merchant-owned accounts to review orders, confirm payment and fulfilment readiness, inspect line items, identify exceptions, and track unfinished work. The discovery context involved approximately eight merchant accounts and 24 recurring account and workflow checks per day.

I independently built and deployed StoreOps Copilot, an AI-assisted multi-merchant fulfilment operations portfolio prototype. It creates an actionable unified order queue, merchant backlog views, explainable exceptions, and a daily operations brief while preserving provider, merchant, and store context on every record. Deterministic rules calculate ageing, payment blocks, partial fulfilment, inventory constraints, backlog risk, and operational priority. The optional OpenAI path receives only scoped, pre-calculated facts; schema and exact evidence validation reject altered or cross-merchant output and fall back safely.

I used AI coding tools throughout product framing, implementation, debugging, testing, and documentation while retaining responsibility for scope, rule design, safeguards, technical validation, and quality. This is independent portfolio work inspired by discovery—not a client deliverable, production deployment, measured outcome, or Shopify-endorsed product.
