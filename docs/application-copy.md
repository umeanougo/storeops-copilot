## Fuller version — under 1,500 characters

While helping a business evaluate its Shopify fulfilment operations, I observed a recurring workflow problem: operators had plenty of data, but still had to reconcile several screens to decide what needed attention, why it mattered, and what to do next. I independently built StoreOps Copilot, an AI-assisted portfolio prototype that turns Shopify operational records into explainable daily priorities.

The prototype uses deterministic rules for overdue orders, low and excess inventory, customer risk, refund activity, severity, and priority. The optional OpenAI path is constrained to an exact source-derived claim and evidence allowlist; altered output falls back safely, while unsupported questions are declined without invented citations. I used AI coding tools throughout problem framing, implementation, refactoring, testing, debugging, and documentation, while retaining responsibility for product scope, rule design, technical validation, trade-offs, and quality.

This is independently built portfolio work inspired by workflow discovery. It is not a client-owned deliverable, production Shopify app, measured merchant deployment, or Shopify-endorsed product.
